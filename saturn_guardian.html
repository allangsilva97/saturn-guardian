-- SUBCLASS --
Skill wins fights. Headshots are critical hits (+75%, sniper +150%). [Shift] dashes through shots; dodging a hit that way is a PERFECT DODGE: +25% damage for 3s and the dash is refunded. You have a few lives per stage (2 on Normal) and every champion you defeat grants one more: dying sends you back to the last checkpoint and costs score and credits, and losing the last life fails the mission. Match your element to a shield's colour to shatter it. Melee breaks any shield. Shieldbearers block shots from the front, so flank them or use explosives. Crouch to duck under level shots. Champions (max 2 types per stage, shown at the start): BARRIER → Piercer Rifle; OVERLOAD → Arc Disruptor; UNSTOPPABLE → Breacher Slug (Armory). Ride elevators to tower roofs for treasure, and look for cracked walls hiding secret stashes. Hide: crouch behind low walls or stand inside a shelter and enemies lose track of you; firing gives you away. Later stages of a world are rougher: ledges crumble and debris falls (watch the shadows).
